# How to update your website

A step-by-step guide for the team at Sunshine Coast Oral, Facial & Implant Specialists.

**Sign in:** go to **sunshinecoastofis.com.au/admin** and sign in with the email and password GYA set up for you. If you forget your password, ask GYA to reset it.

**The golden rule:** changes are made in two steps.

1. **Save** the thing you changed (a page, an article, the settings). Saving keeps your change in the dashboard but does not change the website yet.
2. **Publish website** (the button on the Dashboard home screen) when you are ready. The website rebuilds with every saved change and is live in two to three minutes.

So you can make several changes, check them with **Preview**, and publish them all at once.

---

## Before you write anything: AHPRA advertising rules

The website advertises a regulated health service, so AHPRA's rules apply to everything you type in.

- No testimonials or patient reviews about clinical care, and no quoting reviews.
- No claims such as "pain-free", "painless", "guaranteed", "best", "leading" or "expert", and nothing that promises a result.
- Any offer must state the price, what is included and the period it runs for.
- Use "specialist" only for Dr Trent Lincoln and Dr Andrew Higgins as registered specialist oral and maxillofacial surgeons. Never call the practice team, a treatment or anyone else a specialist.
- Keep articles to general information, and say that surgery carries risks and that patients should seek a second opinion where appropriate.

If you are unsure, ask GYA before publishing.

## 1. Edit the wording on a page

The page editor shows the real page on the left, exactly as visitors see it, with a panel on the right.

1. Click **Pages** in the left menu.
2. Click the page, for example **Wisdom teeth removal**. The header, menu and footer (shown on every page) are under **Header, menu and footer**.
3. **Click any text on the page** and type. A dashed outline shows what can be edited. Press Enter for a new line in paragraphs; in headings and buttons, Enter finishes editing.
4. In paragraphs you can make words **bold** (Ctrl+B, or Cmd+B on a Mac) or *italic* (Ctrl+I or Cmd+I).
5. Words on a menu or form button open a small box instead, so the button does not get pressed while you type.
6. Use **Desktop** and **Mobile** at the top to see the page as it looks on a computer or a phone.
7. Click **Save changes** (top right).
8. When you are happy, go to **Dashboard** and click **Publish website**.

**Change where a button or link goes:** click the button on the page, then **Change link** in the small dark bar above it, and pick a page (start typing its name) or paste a full https:// address. Every button and link on the page is also listed in the panel under **Content > Buttons and links**.

**The phone number** in buttons and wording comes from Site Settings. You can edit the words around it on the page; to change the number itself, use **Site Settings > Practice**, and it changes everywhere.

**Some parts come from elsewhere** and are edited there, so every page stays the same: the surgeon bios and team photos (**Team**), the hospital list and map (**Site Settings > Locations**) and the article cards (**Blog Posts**). Clicking them on the page tells you where to go.

**The Sections tab** (right panel):

- **Hide a section** without deleting it: use the **Shown** switch. Switch it back on to bring it back.
- **Move a section:** drag the six-dot handle up or down. (Keyboard: focus the handle, press Space, use the arrow keys, press Space again.)
- **Open a section** to see all its wording, links and photos as a form. Handy for anything that is hard to click on the page, such as the mobile menu.

**Preview** (top bar) opens the page with your changes in a new tab, full size.

The layout, colours and fonts of the approved design are fixed, so the website always stays on brand. If you need a new type of section, ask GYA.

## 2. Swap a photo

**Change a photo on a page:**

1. Open the page (Pages, then the page).
2. **Click the photo on the page** (the pointer shows "Click to replace this photo"), or click **Replace** next to it in the panel under **Content > Photos on this page**.
3. Pick a photo from the library, or use the **Upload** tab to add one from your computer.
4. Describe the photo in a few words in the panel (for example "Consulting room at the Buderim practice"). This description is read out to people who use screen readers and helps Google.
5. Click **Save changes**, then **Publish website**.

**Replace a photo everywhere it is used** (for example a new photo of the reception):

1. Click **Media Library**.
2. Click the photo, then **Replace this photo** and choose the new file.
3. Every page that uses that photo shows the new one after you click **Publish website**.

Photos are resized automatically, so a photo straight from a phone or camera is fine. Use landscape photos for page banners.

**Team photos:** click **Team**, click **Edit** on the person, click the photo, choose or upload the new one, then **Save** and **Publish website**.

## 3. Write a blog post

1. Click **Blog Posts**, then **New article**.
2. Read the AHPRA reminder at the top.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: the headline.
   - **Summary**: one or two sentences shown on the blog page under the title.
   - **Article**: the article itself. Use **H2** for sub-headings, the list buttons for lists, **Link** to link words to another page.
   - **Featured image** (right): click **Choose / Upload** and pick a photo.
   - **Publish date**: today, unless you want a different date shown.
   - **Web address**: filled in from the title. Leave it unless GYA asks you to change it.
   - **Categories**: pick one, or add a new one.
4. Open the **SEO** part of the article to set the Google title and description (see section 6). If left empty, the title and summary are used.
5. Click **Save draft** to keep working later, or **Publish article** when it is ready.
6. Go to **Dashboard** and click **Publish website**. The article appears on the blog page and at its own address.

To take an article down, open it and unpublish it (or delete it), then **Publish website**.

## 4. Update the opening hours

The hours appear in two places, and both should match:

1. **What Google reads:** click **Site Settings**, then the **Opening hours** tab. Set each day's open and close times, or tick **Closed**. Click **Save settings**.
2. **What visitors read:** the hours wording on the website is part of the page wording. Click **Pages**, open **Header, menu and footer** for the footer, and **Contact us** for the Contact page. Click the hours on the page, type the new times, click **Save changes**.
3. Click **Publish website**.

For a public holiday or a one-off closure, change the wording on the page (step 2) and change it back afterwards.

## 5. Update a hospital location

The hospital cards on the Locations page, the list on the homepage map and the details Google reads all come from one list.

1. Click **Site Settings**, then the **Locations** tab.
2. Open the hospital. You can change its name, the small area label, the address, the short note shown when it is selected, the **Get directions** link and what the map looks up.
3. To stop showing a hospital without deleting it, tick **Hide**.
4. To add a hospital, click **+ Add a location** and fill in the details, including **Pin on the coast map** to choose where it sits on the map.
5. Click **Save settings**, then **Publish website**.

The practice's own address and phone number are on the **Practice** tab. Changing the phone number there changes it in every button, the header and the footer.

## 6. Change the SEO (what Google shows)

**For one page:**

1. Open the page (Pages, then the page) and click the **SEO** tab in the right panel.
2. **Meta title**: the blue link in Google and the browser tab. Keep it under 60 characters.
3. **Meta description**: the two lines under the link in Google. Aim for 70 to 155 characters and say what the page is about.
4. The preview shows roughly how it may look in Google, and the counters turn orange or red when it is too long.
5. **Share title**, **Share description** and **Share image** are what Facebook and messaging apps show when someone shares the page. Leave them empty to use the meta title, description and the page's main photo.
6. Leave **Hide this page from search engines** unticked unless GYA says otherwise.
7. Click **Save changes**, then **Publish website**.

**See every page at once:** click **SEO** in the left menu. It lists every page with its title and description, and shows **Missing** where one is empty.

The page addresses (URLs) match the old website so Google keeps your rankings. Please do not change a page's address; ask GYA if you need one changed, because it needs a redirect.

## 7. Publish the website

1. Click **Dashboard**.
2. The box at the top says whether there are saved changes waiting to go live.
3. Click **Publish website**. The website rebuilds and your changes are live in two to three minutes.
4. If something looks wrong afterwards, change it back, save, and publish again.

## Enquiries

Every form sent from the website (Contact us, Online doctor referral, Patient information form and Feedback) is emailed to the practice and also listed under **Enquiries**, newest first.

- Click an enquiry to see everything that was sent. Tick **Followed up** once someone has called or replied, and add **Notes** for the team.
- **Export to CSV** downloads the list for a spreadsheet.
- For privacy, the medical details from the Patient information form are only in the email, not stored in the dashboard.

## Team

Click **Team**. **Surgeons** holds Dr Lincoln's and Dr Higgins's bios; **Practice team** holds the rest of the team.

- Click **Edit** on a person's row to change their name, role or photo. Click **Save**.
- Drag the handle to change the order they appear in.
- Click **Hide** on the person's row to take them off the website without deleting them (**Show** brings them back).
- Click **Add a team member** for a new starter.
- Then **Publish website**.

## Need help?

Contact GYA. Tell us the page, what you changed and what you expected to see. A screenshot helps.
